# StockTrack (Inventory Monitor App)
## Operations & Maintenance Manual

This manual is a practical guide for operating StockTrack in a retail store. It covers products, stock movements, sales, customer credit, store activity, and routine maintenance. App labels may vary slightly by release.

To create a PDF copy from the app, open **Help & Tutorials**, search for **manual**, expand **Operations & Maintenance Manual**, then select **Print / Save PDF Copy**. In a web browser, choose **Save as PDF** in the print dialog. On a phone or tablet, use the share sheet to save or send the PDF.

## 1. Purpose and responsibilities

Use this manual as a practical guide for operating StockTrack (Inventory Monitor App) in a retail store. The app records products, stock movements, sales, customer credit, and store activity; staff remain responsible for entering accurate information and reconciling it with physical stock and cash. Store administrators should assign each user an appropriate role, train staff before they transact, and review important records regularly.

## 2. Opening the store

Sign in with your own account and confirm you are working in the correct store. Check the dashboard for low-stock and out-of-stock items, alerts, and recent activity. Review any pending offline sync changes in Settings before relying on records from another device. If your store uses cash shifts, open the register from **Profile → Store → Cash Shifts & Drawer** and enter the actual starting float.

## 3. Sales, payments, and customer credit

At checkout, select or scan each product, verify the item and quantity, review the cart, and select the payment method shown by the customer. Enter the amount received accurately and complete the sale before handing over the receipt. Record customer credit in Credit Tracker against the correct customer, then record each repayment against its credit record. Use Sales History to review a transaction; only authorized users should void a mistaken sale, and the reason should be recorded.

## 4. Receiving deliveries and controlling stock

Compare each delivery with its supplier order and invoice, count and inspect the items, and record only the quantities actually received. Purchase orders can be received in multiple deliveries; draft orders do not change stock. For deliveries without a purchase order, use Stock In and record supplier or invoice details when available. Record damaged, expired, missing, or corrected quantities with Stock Out and the truthful reason—do not use an adjustment to hide a discrepancy. Keep batch and expiry details current for products that use FEFO tracking.

## 5. Closing and daily reconciliation

Review Sales History and compare recorded transactions with cash and e-wallet receipts. If a cash shift is open, count the physical drawer and use **End Shift & Count Drawer**; investigate any shortage or overage instead of changing figures to force a match. Review stock movements, alerts, and any unusual changes. When the device has been offline, restore connectivity and confirm pending changes have synced before ending the day.

## 6. Reports and record keeping

Use Reports to review weekly or monthly activity and inventory turnover. Export inventory or activity logs from Reports or Profile as CSV, Word, or a printable report where offered. Store exported records in a business-approved, access-controlled location. Exports are useful snapshots, but they are not a complete system backup and do not replace required accounting or statutory records.

## 7. Offline use and synchronization

Some work can be queued while a device is offline, but other devices may not show those changes until synchronization succeeds. Reconnect to a reliable network, open **Settings → Alerts and Sync → Offline Sync Queue**, tap **Sync Now**, and review the synced, failed, and remaining counts. Resolve the specific failure and retry. Never clear the queue unless you have confirmed the pending changes are no longer needed; clearing it permanently discards them.

## 8. Routine maintenance

- **Daily:** Review stock alerts, pending purchase orders, expiry warnings, and sync status.
- **Weekly:** Verify fast-moving and high-value items against physical counts, check upcoming expiry dates, and review unusual stock adjustments.
- **Monthly and whenever costs change:** Review product names, barcodes, categories, cost and selling prices, low-stock thresholds, and staff access. Remove or update obsolete catalog entries only after checking their transaction history.
- Keep devices charged, operating systems and the installed app reasonably up to date, and exported business records protected.

## 9. Account and information security

Use individual accounts; do not share passwords or leave a signed-in device unattended. Give staff only the access they need and remove access when a worker leaves. Use a device screen lock and protect exported reports, customer details, and receipts from unauthorized access. Never put passwords, verification codes, or backend service keys into support messages. Contact the store administrator if your access or store assignment looks wrong.

## 10. Troubleshooting and support

For a missing or unexpected transaction, check the selected store, Sales History, stock activity, and the pending sync queue before entering it again; repeating an operation can create duplicates. For an incorrect stock count, compare the product's recent sales and stock movements with the physical count, then make a clearly described correction using the appropriate stock action. For barcode problems, confirm the product barcode and allow camera access. For persistent sign-in, invitation, or sync failures, record the error message and contact [stocktracksupport@gmail.com](mailto:stocktracksupport@gmail.com). Do not send your password.
